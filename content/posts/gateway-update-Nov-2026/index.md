The ADI Gateway Cooperative is an Antares-based regional group focused on constructing gateways. By contributing resources to the gateways, members earn ownership shares proportional to their contributions. By banding together, [co-op members ensure the smooth operation of gateways](/adi-gateway-cooperative/) in Antares and beyond!

* Upkeep is the biggest cost of running our gates
* Weekly income keeps climbing
* An ANT to ARC gateway is under construction

## The Cost of Upkeep

{{< twoColImage right="false" imageURL="summary.png" >}}

After 42.9 weeks of operation, the AGC network has earned ₳239.7m in tolls. After upkeep and fuel, net profit is ₳139.3m. **More than 40% of everything our gates have earned has gone back into keeping them running.**

Every gate needs this upkeep shipment each week:

* 15 ALR, 6 WRH, 60 SEA, 6 POW, 15 SP, 60 SPT

That works out to about ₳225k per gate per week. With 10 gates in the network, that's about **₳2.25m every week**. Fuel adds about ₳200k per gate each time it's refilled (10,000 VF at a time), which comes to roughly another ₳800k a week across the network. These prices are still kinda under market price, but are sustainable.

{{< /twoColImage >}}

Against a capital expenditure of ₳3.4 billion, the network currently returns 0.10% per week. At that rate the ROI is still about 20 years. (almost at 1 year down!) Upkeep is a fixed cost, so it doesn't shrink when traffic is slow. Every additional ship through our gates goes straight to the bottom line, but a gate with little traffic can easily cost more to run than it earns.

This matters as we consider new gates. A new gate has to earn at least ₳225k a week plus fuel just to break even. Shareholders should keep that number in mind when evaluating proposals.

## Gateway Income

{{< twoColImage right="true" imageURL="weeklyIncome.png" >}}

The good news is that income keeps growing. Weekly gross income has climbed from about ₳4m in March to around ₳8.5m today. As traffic grows, upkeep becomes a smaller share of income, so each new week of growth improves our margins.

Contributors should keep an eye out for payouts from Hephaestus, Griffonstone, and the other gate planets.

{{< /twoColImage >}}

# ANT to ARC Gateway Under Construction

Pxlfox is building the ANT to ARC gateway [proposed in August](/posts/agc-gateways-august-2026/). So far, **1,946,124.95 of 4,715,814.98 AIC** worth of materials have been collected, about 41% of the total.

When it's complete, the gate is expected to join the [ADI Gateway Cooperative](/adi-gateway-cooperative/) network.
